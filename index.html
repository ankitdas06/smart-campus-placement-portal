Academic Purpose & Scope

Project Objectives & Vision

Engineered as a high-impact, standalone BCA academic project providing seamless placement readiness for tier-2 & tier-3 college candidates.

Structured Recruitment Syllabus

Consolidates all recurring campus exam sections: Arithmetic Aptitude, Analytical Reasoning, Business English, and Core CS Fundamentals.

Real-time Exam Simulation

Incorporates an industry-standard test engine with countdown timer, dynamic question navigation palette, and automatic submission.

Deep Diagnostic Analytics

Generates immediate score breakdowns, subject proficiency bars, accuracy rates, and personalized improvement suggestions.

End-to-End Interview Readiness

Offers exhaustive HR question frameworks, STAR method project explanation guides, and curated Group Discussion (GD) topics.

Dedicated Administration Panel

Empowers placement coordinators and faculty to audit registered students, manage question banks (CRUD), and inspect candidate records.

Zero-Cost LocalStorage Architecture

Operates entirely on client-side HTML5 LocalStorage, requiring no expensive external backend servers or subscription APIs.
